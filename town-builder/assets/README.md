# Placeholder art

The MVP draws its isometric terrain in `scripts/town_map.gd` and building silhouettes
in `scripts/building.gd`. All art is generated by the project; no external art pack is required.

To replace a building, add a transparent PNG under `assets/buildings/` and set
`sprite_path` in `data/buildings.json` to its `res://` path. Art is scaled to 78 pixels
wide and anchored at the bottom centre of its tile. Keep the ground contact there.
The reusable building scene owns this visual, so simulation values are independent of art.

`terrain/`, `icons/`, and `ui/` are reserved for later art. No extra systems are required
to finish MVP 0.1. Use `TileMapLayer` if the map later grows large enough to benefit from an atlas.
